Privacy.

What this site collects, who sees it, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote, hold a unit, book service, request a part or ask about financing, we collect what you type into the form: your name, phone number, and what you told us about your trailer, load or truck.

Who sees it

Your request goes to Timeless Trailers in Waxhaw, NC, and to nobody else. We do not sell or share it.

Cookies and tracking

This site remembers a few things in your own browser, like trailers you saved or compared. We count visits with a privacy-respecting analytics service that does not use cookies.
