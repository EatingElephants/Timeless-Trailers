About the 1420 Split Tilt

14K split tilt for skid steers and mini excavators. The 16-foot tilt section drops while the 4-foot front deck stays put.

Features

  • 16' tilt deck + 4' stationary deck, 82-3/8" wide
  • 22" deck height, 12° loading angle
  • Tandem 7,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • 18 stake pockets
  • Side crank jack, 12,000 lb
  • Adjustable 2-5/16" coupler
  • LED lighting
