About the 1020 EZ Load

10K EZ Load for heavier cars, trucks and compact tractors. L-track on three sides makes tie-down quick.

Features

  • Powered EZ Load deck system, loading angle under 4°
  • 19' 6" x 81" deck, 24" deck height
  • Tandem 5,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • (4) movable D-rings in integrated L-track, front and both sides
  • 4 stake pockets
  • LED lighting
