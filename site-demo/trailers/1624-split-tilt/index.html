About the 1624 Split Tilt

16K split tilt with an 18-foot tilt and a 6-foot front deck, for bigger track loaders and a mini-ex with attachments.

Features

  • 18' tilt deck + 6' stationary deck, 82-3/8" wide
  • 22" deck height, 12° loading angle
  • Tandem 8,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • ST235/80R16G 14-ply tires
  • 18 stake pockets
  • Side crank jack, 12,000 lb
  • LED lighting
