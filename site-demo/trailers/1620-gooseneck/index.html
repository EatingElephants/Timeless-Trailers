About the 1620 Gooseneck

16K gooseneck deck over for longer runs and bigger machines. Toolbox with chain rack, chain tray and spare tire come standard.

Features

  • 20' x 98.25" usable deck
  • 60" dovetail with approx. 4' ramps
  • 35" deck height, 14° loading angle
  • Tandem 7,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • Dual 12,000 lb jacks
  • Toolbox with chain rack, chain tray, spare tire and mount, front bump rail
