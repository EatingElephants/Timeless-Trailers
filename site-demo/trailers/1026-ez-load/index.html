About the 1026 EZ Load

The longest deck in the EZ Load line: 26 feet. A Jeep and a side-by-side, or a car with room to spare.

Features

  • Powered EZ Load deck system, loading angle under 4°
  • 26' x 81" deck, 24" deck height
  • Tandem 5,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • (4) movable D-rings in integrated L-track
  • 8 stake pockets
  • LED lighting
