About the 1420 Deck Over

14K deck over: the full 98-inch deck above the wheels, 7K torsion axles and 50/50 ramps. The lighter-duty way into a deck over.

Features

  • 20' x 98.25" usable deck over the wheels
  • 60" dovetail with approx. 4' 50/50 ramps
  • 35" deck height, 14° loading angle
  • Tandem 7,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • 24 stake pockets
  • Side crank jack, 12,000 lb
