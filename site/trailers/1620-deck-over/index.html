About the 1620 Deck Over

16K deck over: the full 98-inch deck sits above the wheels, with a 5-foot dovetail and 50/50 ramps. Updated design for 2026.

Features

  • 20' x 98.25" usable deck over the wheels
  • 60" dovetail with approx. 4' 50/50 ramps
  • 35" deck height, 14° loading angle
  • Tandem 8,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • 24 stake pockets
  • 2026 update: up to 520 lb more payload than the previous model
