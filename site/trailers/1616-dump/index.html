About the 1616 Dump

16K aluminum dump with a 16-foot box and 8K axles: the most room and the most payload of the dump line.

Features

  • Aluminum construction with no structural welds
  • 16' x 82" box, 24" sides
  • Dexter scissor hoist
  • Barn door / spreader combo rear gate
  • Full-length mesh tarp
  • A-frame toolbox, spare tire and mount
  • Rear stabilizers
  • Tandem 8,000 lb rubber torsion axles
