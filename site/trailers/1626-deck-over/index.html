About the 1626 Deck Over

16K deck over with a 25-foot-4 deck and 8K torsion axles: the longest bumper-pull deck over in the line, for bigger machines and long loads.

Features

  • 25' 4" x 98.25" usable deck over the wheels
  • 60" dovetail with approx. 4' 50/50 ramps
  • 35" deck height, 14° loading angle
  • Tandem 8,000 lb rubber torsion axles
  • 28 stake pockets
  • Side crank jack, 12,000 lb
  • 2026 update: up to 520 lb more payload than the previous model
